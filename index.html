Getting Started

Up & running in 3 steps

No accounts. No apps. Just Excel or Google Sheets.

01

Download the Master Sheet

Click the download button above or below. You'll get a single .xlsx file containing all 2,192 entries across 10 organised sheets — one for each subject category plus a master "All Past Papers" sheet and a summary.

Works with: Excel · Google Sheets · LibreOffice · Numbers

02

Filter by Subject, Year & Type

Use Excel's built-in AutoFilter on the Subject, Year, or Document Type columns. Instantly narrow 2,192 rows to exactly what you need — for example, all Biology marking guides from 2022–2025, or every Specialist Maths exam paper ever released.

Tip: Use Ctrl+Shift+L to toggle AutoFilter on any sheet

03

Click Any Link to Open the PDF

Every row contains a live direct URL pointing straight to the official QCAA PDF. Click it to open the document in your browser — no login, no redirect. Works for exam papers, marking guides, stimulus books, syllabuses, and more.

Links sourced directly from qcaa.qld.edu.au

Spreadsheet Structure

Inside Every Row

Each of the 2,192 entries contains exactly six fields, designed to be instantly filterable and sortable in any spreadsheet application.

A
Subject

The full official name of the QCE subject (e.g. Mathematical Methods, Biology, Film, Television & New Media).

B
Category

The broad subject area — one of 8 categories: Sciences, Humanities & HASS, Mathematics, English, Languages, Technologies, The Arts, or Health & PE.

C
Year

The academic year of publication. Range covers 2018 through 2026, with the majority of exam material from 2019 onward (post-QCAA reform).

D
Document Type

One of 11 types: Exam Paper, Exam Paper (Multiple Choice), Marking Guide, Sample Assessment Instrument, Subject Report, Stimulus Book, Sample Student Response, Syllabus, Formula Book, Memo, or Other Resource.

E
Document Title

The full descriptive name of the document as it appears on the QCAA website (e.g. Paper 1 — Multiple choice question book).

F
URL

A live, direct hyperlink to the official QCAA PDF or resource. Clicking takes you straight to the file — no intermediate pages or login screens.

Open Source & Community

Built in public.
Free forever.

This entire project — the archive data, this website, and all tooling — is hosted openly on GitHub Pages. There is no business model, no premium tier, and no plans to monetise. Every Queensland student deserves equal access to past exam materials.

Permanently Free

Hosted on GitHub Pages — zero hosting costs passed to users. The repository is public and can be forked, mirrored, or re-hosted by anyone.

Open Data

The master spreadsheet is the canonical data source. Developers can build study tools, flashcard apps, or revision schedulers on top of this data without restriction.

Community Contributions

Spotted a missing paper or a broken link? Pull requests welcome. The more contributors, the more complete and reliable this resource becomes for every QCE student in Queensland.
