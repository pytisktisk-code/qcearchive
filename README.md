# 🇦🇺 QCAA Past Papers Archive (Aussie Made, Baby!) 🚀

Welcome to the ultimate, zero-nonsense, completely open-source repository for Queensland Curriculum and Assessment Authority (QCAA) past papers. 

Built right here in Australia for Queensland seniors, this project exists to tear down the paywalls, eliminate the annoying sign-up screens, and give every student across the Sunshine State instant access to the study resources they need.

---

## 💎 The Philosophy: Simple & Free
Most resource hubs force you to make an account, log in with school emails, or watch endless ads just to look at a math exam from three years ago. Bugger that. 

This platform is a single-page hub containing a master archive. You click one button, you get the entire dataset in a beautifully structured Excel sheet, and you are ready to smash your QCE.

* **No Sign-ups:** Just open and download.
* **No Tracking:** Your data is your own.
* **100% Free & Open Source:** Built by the community, for the community.

---

## 📁 Project Structure

This repository is kept intentionally lightweight so it loads instantly on school Wi-Fi or mobile data:

```text
├── index.html                       # The single-page Tailwind CSS frontend
├── qcaa_past_papers_master.xlsx     # The holy grail: The Master Excel spreadsheet
└── README.md                        # You are here!

🛠️ How It Works
Grab the Sheet: Hit the massive download button on the live site to get qcaa_past_papers_master.xlsx.

Filter Your Subjects: Open it up in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers. Use the built-in filter arrows on the top row to sort instantly by Subject, Year, or Unit.

Click & Study: Click the direct URLs embedded in the sheet to instantly pull up the exact past paper or marking guide you need.

🤝 Contributing (Keen to Help Out?)
Got your hands on a missing paper or noticed a broken link in the master spreadsheet? Let's fix it! Since this is a fully open-source project, anyone can contribute:

Fork this repository.

Update the qcaa_past_papers_master.xlsx file with the new data.

Submit a Pull Request explaining what you added.

Let's keep this archive growing for the next generation of Year 11s and 12s.
